# WordPress Industry Direction 2026 — Fable 5 take (fresh research)

> **DRAFT.** Research re-run independently by Fable 5 (July 11, 2026) — own queries, live retrieval,
> not reusing the Opus 4.8 research set. New findings vs. the Opus round are marked **🆕**.

## 1. Read the decline correctly

- 43.2% (Dec 2025) → **41.9% (July 2026)**, six consecutive quarters of decline, pace accelerating —
  the 6-month drop equals double all of 2025's.
- **The tell:** losses go mostly to **"no CMS detected,"** not to a rival — WordPress is losing the
  *inflow of trivial sites* to AI builders, not its installed base.
- 🆕 **Perspective check:** WordPress at 41.9% is still **~9× its nearest competitor** (Shopify 5.2%,
  Wix 4.3%). Mainstream tech press has noticed ("Automattic's CMS empire shows cracks" — The Register),
  so the *narrative* pressure is now real too — expect customers to ask "is WordPress dying?"

## 2. The shocks, re-examined with fresh data

| Signal | What the fresh round adds |
|---|---|
| Elementor −30% staff | CEO: "we underestimated how fast AI disruption would move… the old model." 🆕 **Wix cut 20% a month earlier** — it's an industry-wide builder reset, not one company stumbling. |
| Content traffic collapse | 🆕 Worse than round one suggested: **80%+ of searches now end without any click** (up from the ~60% figure); publisher traffic from Google fell **~⅓ in 2025** alone. 🆕 Crucial split: informational queries ~**74% zero-click** vs transactional ~**31%** — *informational content is now a citation play; transactional/product pages still get clicks.* |
| WP 7.0/7.1 core AI | 🆕 **7.1 lands August 19, 2026 (beta July 15 — days away)** with AI as a named focus: queryable/filterable Abilities API, curated core abilities, AI Client + connector improvements. Core's stated goal: AI "native, not a plugin afterthought." The commoditization clock is faster than round one assumed. |

## 3. 🆕 Two findings the Opus round didn't surface

1. **The plugin market is flooding, and commercial plugin sales are declining.** wp.org submissions hit
   ~**700/week in early 2026 — ~5× the 2024 pace** — largely AI-generated, with untested "ghost" plugins
   eroding buyer trust; meanwhile established sellers report declining sales as core absorbs features
   and agencies replace plugins with AI-written custom code. **Implication:** discovery via the
   directory is dying as a growth channel; *trust* (brand, track record, reviews, being cited by AI)
   becomes the scarce asset. A 5-star maintained plugin with years of history is now a moat in itself.
2. **The niching consensus.** Multiple 2026 analyses converge: generic plugins stagnate; *sharply
   positioned products for specific audiences* dominate. This independently validates the
   vertical/up-the-stack strategy — it's not just our read, it's the ecosystem's read.

## 4. The career question — unchanged, now better-armed

Do **not** retrain as an ML researcher / data scientist (multi-year pivot into a labs-dominated field,
abandoning a 12-year WordPress moat). Do become an **applied-AI product engineer** — embeddings, RAG,
prompt/tool design, evals, agents, on-device inference. You already ship most of these pieces. Don't
self-host foundation models; rent intelligence, own the product. Fresh data strengthens this: with
core shipping the AI plumbing (7.0) and improving it *quarterly* (7.1 in August), the value of building
plumbing yourself approaches zero — the value of product judgment on top of it grows.

## 5. Recommendation (final — sharpened by fresh data)

**Adapt in place. Move above the plumbing. Move fast — the clock is quarterly, not yearly.**

1. **Connector** = flagship, rebuilt on core's Abilities/AI Client — target **7.1 (Aug 19)** as the
   alignment deadline, not "someday." Fix the license stub first.
2. **AtlasVoice** = revenue engine — accessibility is wp.org-promoted, buyers sticky; and per the
   zero-click split, its buyers arrive via *product/transactional* searches that still convert.
3. **Smart Local AI** = differentiator — on-device privacy, the thing core and the labs won't give away.
4. **AtlasAR** = vertical bet (WooCommerce product pages are transactional — the surviving click category).
5. **Marketing pivots from ranking to being cited** — informational content only as citation bait;
   invest in structured data, entities, and the **trust signals** (reviews, track record) that both AI
   systems and flood-wary buyers now select for.

## 6. One line

> The directory is flooding, the plumbing is free, and the informational funnel is gone — what's left
> to sell is **trust and vertical judgment**, and a 12-year founder with four maintained products has
> more of both than almost anyone entering now.

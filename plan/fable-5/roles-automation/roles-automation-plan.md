# Roles Automation Plan — Fable 5 take

> **Status: DRAFT.** Original prompt: "I perform ~14 roles as a solo founder; how do I automate,
> delegate to AI, templatize, and what stays human-only? What is the most important thing?"

## The decision ladder

Don't sort roles into static buckets first — sort **tasks** as they arrive, with one ladder you apply
in order every time something lands on your desk:

1. **Can a machine do it on a schedule?** → Automate it once, never see it again.
2. **Can AI produce a reviewable draft?** → Delegate; your job shrinks to a 5-minute review.
3. **Will this exact shape recur?** → Template it now; the second occurrence pays for the first.
4. **None of the above** → It's yours. Do it during your protected hours.

A *role* is just the pattern of where its tasks keep landing on this ladder.

## Where the 14 roles land

| Role | Ladder home | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Founder / Owner | 4 — yours | The only role with no fallback. |
| Software Architect | 4 (AI drafts, you decide) | Decisions are yours; exploration is delegable. |
| Software Engineer | 3–4 | Core features yours; boilerplate/AI pair for the rest. |
| Bug Fixer / Debugger | 2 | Describe → AI investigates → you verify the fix live. |
| Product Manager | 3–4 | Direction yours; ticket writing templated. |
| QA / Test Engineer | 1 | Checklist gate + "every fixed bug earns a test." |
| Release Manager | 1 + final button | Script everything; only *ship* stays manual. |

| Technical Writer | 2 | Near-fully delegable. |
| Content Writer | 2 | Near-fully delegable. |

| Email / Outreach | 1 + 2 | AI drafts, scheduler sends, you approve sends. |
| Business Analyst | 1 | Scheduled report lands in your inbox. |
| Support / Community | 2 + human warmth | AI drafts every reply; you humanize and send. |

## The operating rhythm (the part bucket-lists usually miss)

Buckets tell you *what*; a calendar tells you *when*. Suggested weekly shape:

- **Protected mornings (×4/week):** core engineering + architecture only. No support, no marketing.
- **One admin afternoon:** review all AI drafts queued that week (replies, content, reports) in one batch.
  Batching reviews is the single biggest time win — context-switching is what actually costs you 14 roles.
- **One planning hour:** founder work — direction, pricing, what not to build.
- **Everything scheduled** (reports, sequences, reminders) fires into a queue, not into your attention.

## Most important thing

**Protecting deep engineering + direction time is the point; the buckets are just the fence around it.**
The 13 other roles exist to be compressed so that role #1 (building the product only you can build)
gets whole, uninterrupted days.

> ⚖ Divergence from Opus 4.8: same four buckets and largely the same assignments — the genuine
> difference is emphasis. Opus framed it as *classification* (sort the roles); Fable frames it as
> *cadence* (batch the reviews, protect the hours). Classification without a calendar quietly fails,
> because interruption — not workload — is what makes 14 roles unsustainable.

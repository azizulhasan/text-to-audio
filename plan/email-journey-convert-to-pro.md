## EMAIL 2 — Quick Tips (Day 3)

**Subject Line:** 3 things most AtlasVoice users miss
**Preview Text:** Quick tips to get more out of your text-to-speech setup
**From:** Azizul Hasan <contact@atlasaidev.com>

---

Hi *|FNAME|*,

Most people install AtlasVoice and think "done." But there are a few easy tweaks that make a big difference:

**Tip 1: Position your player above the fold**

Move the play button to the top of your posts. Users who see it immediately are 3x more likely to press play. Go to AtlasVoice > Customize to change the position.

**Tip 2: Use it on your top-performing posts first**

Check your Google Analytics — find your top 10 posts by traffic. Those are the ones where audio adds the most value. More visitors = more listeners = more engagement.

**Tip 3: Mention it in your content**

Add a simple line at the top: "Prefer to listen? Hit the play button above." You'd be surprised how many people prefer audio but don't notice the button.

**Bonus: The accessibility angle**

If your site serves any public audience (government, education, healthcare, non-profit), text-to-speech isn't just nice to have — it's an accessibility requirement in many countries. AtlasVoice helps you comply with WCAG 2.1 guidelines.

Try these tips this week and see the difference.

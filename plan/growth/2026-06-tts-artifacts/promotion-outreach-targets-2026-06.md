## 💸 Google Merchant / paid-ads question — honest take
**Short answer: Merchant Center (Shopping ads) is NOT the right first paid test for a software plugin.**
- **Eligibility blockers right now:** you can only advertise **Approved** products — 3 of yours are
  "Not approved" (still crawling) and AtlasVoice is "Limited." Ads can't run until that clears.
- **Software-subscription rules:** Shopping ads for subscriptions require the **product title to literally
  include "(1-year subscription)"**, yearly prepaid term, and shipping = $0 (shipping ✓ already). Your
  titles don't, so they'd be disapproved for ads as-is.
- **ROI reality:** Shopping ads are built for physical retail; for a plugin, a small budget goes further
  in **Google Search ads** on high-intent keywords (e.g., "wordpress text to speech plugin",
  "elevenlabs wordpress", "google cloud tts wordpress") — OR in a WPGlob/LearnWoo review, which is a
  **durable, citable asset** (an ad vanishes the moment you stop paying; a ranked review keeps working).
- **If you still want a paid test:** put the same small budget into **(a) a WPGlob review/listicle**
  (recommended) or **(b) a tiny Google *Search* Ads test ($5–10/day)** on 3–5 exact-match keywords.
  Keep Merchant/Shopping for later, only after products are approved + titles reformatted.
- **Note:** launching any ad campaign commits real spend + billing — that's the founder's action to
  confirm/enter; I can help configure the campaign, keywords, and budget caps, but won't commit spend.

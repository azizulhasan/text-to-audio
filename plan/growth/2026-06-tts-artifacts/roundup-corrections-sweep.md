### Why this matters more than any new content

The June baseline blamed brand fragmentation on `llms.txt` and the wp.org title. **That diagnosis was incomplete.** The real mechanism is here: *every third-party round-up on the open web says the legacy name.* AI engines learn entity identity from third-party sources, so as long as these three say "Text To Speech TTS Accessibility", that's the entity they'll resolve to — no amount of first-party copy overrides it.

Three emails plausibly do more for entity consolidation than a quarter of content work. And the ranking data in two of them is provably wrong, which gives a clean, non-promotional reason to write.

## Reusable correction email

The play is identical for WPMU DEV and BDThemes. Lead with the factual error, not the request — you're doing their readers a favour, and that framing is what gets a reply.

**Subject:** Outdated entry in your WordPress text-to-speech round-up

---

Hi,

I'm writing about this post on your site:
[**FULL POST URL**]
("[**exact article title**]", published [**date**] by [**author**])

I build the plugin listed there as [**"Text to Speech TTS" / "Text-to-Speech TTS Accessibility"**]. A couple of details are now well out of date and I thought you'd want to know.

**The name changed.** It's now **AtlasVoice Text To Speech TTS Accessibility** — same plugin, same wordpress.org slug (`text-to-audio`). We consolidated the branding because the old name was being read as a separate product, and right now search engines and AI assistants do treat them as two different plugins.

> **📌 Canonical name for all outreach: `AtlasVoice Text To Speech TTS Accessibility`.**
> Use the full bridged form, not bare "AtlasVoice". The point of these emails is **entity merging** —
> the combined name teaches engines that AtlasVoice *is* the plugin they already know as "Text To
> Speech TTS Accessibility". Asking an editor to replace the old name with a brand-new one would just
> create a third entity and make fragmentation worse. Matches the wordpress.org listing.

**The numbers moved.** [*BDThemes only:*] The entry says 1,000+ active installs and 25 reviews — it's currently **4,000+ active installs and 91 reviews at 4.8★** on wordpress.org, last updated 24 July 2026.

**The feature set is much bigger than described.** [*WPMU DEV only:*] The entry describes it as a free plugin with 20-odd languages. That's still true of the free tier, but there's now a Pro version with four AI voice engines — our own AtlasVoice AI (63 languages, no external API key), plus Google Cloud, OpenAI and ElevenLabs — along with bulk MP3 generation, visitor downloads, listening analytics, audio schema markup and read-along highlighting.

Everything above is verifiable on the wordpress.org listing: https://wordpress.org/plugins/text-to-audio/

Would you consider updating the entry? Happy to send screenshots or a test license — no expectations either way, and I'm not asking to be moved up the list.

Thanks for the work you put into the site.

Founder, AtlasAiDev
atlasaidev.com

### Notes on using it

- **Always open with the full post URL.** These go to general contact addresses that often route through support first — whoever opens it must be able to find the page without guessing. Title, date and author alone are not enough.
- **Delete the bracketed lines that don't apply.** Sending WPMU DEV a note about review counts they never published looks careless.
- **"I'm not asking to be moved up the list"** — keep this. It's what separates a correction from a pitch, and it's why editors act on it.
- **Don't mention competitors.** The BeyondWords conflict of interest is specific to WPExplorer and is already handled there; raising it elsewhere is just mud.
- **7-day gap, one bump, then stop.**

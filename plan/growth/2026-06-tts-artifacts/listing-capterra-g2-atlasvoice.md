## 5. Full description (main body — B2B framing, makes the WordPress context explicit)
AtlasVoice (also published on WordPress.org as "Text To Speech TTS Accessibility") is a text-to-speech
plugin for WordPress that converts written content into natural-sounding audio. Publishers, bloggers,
and accessibility-focused teams use it to add a one-click "Listen" player to posts, pages, and custom
post types — increasing time-on-page, accessibility compliance, and audience reach.

It works free out of the box with built-in browser and Google Translate voices (no API key required).
The Pro edition adds premium AI voice providers and converts content into downloadable, cacheable MP3
files for fast, reliable playback:

- **AtlasVoice AI** — built-in, free, 63 languages
- **Google Cloud TTS** — 300+ neural voices, 90+ languages, SSML support
- **OpenAI / ChatGPT TTS** — 6 HD voices
- **ElevenLabs** — 100+ ultra-realistic voices, 29 languages

Capabilities:
- MP3 generation + bulk export across your entire content library
- Listening analytics with engagement tracking and heatmaps
- Audio schema markup for SEO rich results
- Six customizable player styles plus a sticky/floating player
- CSS-selector targeting to include/exclude specific content
- Audio intros & outros for branding
- Pronunciation aliases to control how names/acronyms are read aloud
- 51+ languages and multilingual sites
- WCAG accessibility compliance
- Compatible with all major page builders and caching plugins (no performance penalty)

Built and supported by AtlasAiDev. Free version available; Pro from $59/yr (or $199 lifetime) with a
14-day money-back guarantee.

## 9. Business Decisions Based on Data

### Decision 1: Focus All Resources on TTS (Single-Product Strategy)

**Data basis:** TTS = 99.2% of installs. AtlasAR, AR Try-On combined = 0.5%.

**Decision:** Stop investing development time in AtlasAR and AR Try-On. Redirect those resources entirely to TTS free + pro. These experimental products have had 12+ months to gain traction and failed. Every hour spent on a plugin with 7 installs is an hour not spent improving the product that feeds the business.

**Exception:** AtlasVoice is too new (Mar 2026) — give it a 6-month evaluation window before deciding.

---

### Decision 2: Treat the Churn Problem as an Emergency

**Data basis:** Losing 300-400 net users per month. Churn ratio is 4-5x. At this rate, the active user base will continue declining.

**Decision:** Shift from "feature development" mode to "retention mode" for the next 2-3 months. This means:
- Freeze all new feature work temporarily
- Dedicate 100% of dev cycles to stability, compatibility, and onboarding
- Goal: reduce the churn ratio from 4x to under 2x

**Rationale:** Acquiring new users is expensive (SEO, marketing, reviews). Retaining existing users costs far less. Fixing a leaking bucket before pouring more water in.

---

### Decision 3: Build an Onboarding Wizard That Actually Works

**Data basis:** 592 users (4.7%) explicitly said "could not understand." This is likely the tip of the iceberg — many of the 56% silent churners probably had the same problem but didn't say so.

**Decision:** Build a mandatory first-run wizard that:
1. Auto-detects the user's TTS use case (blog reader, accessibility, podcast)
2. Configures optimal settings in 3 clicks
3. Shows a live preview of TTS working on their actual content
4. Takes under 60 seconds to complete

**Expected impact:** If this converts even 10% of the "could not understand" and "silent churn" users, that's ~750+ retained users per year.

---

### Decision 4: Investigate and Fix the June 2024 Cliff

**Data basis:** Installs dropped 60% in one month (354 to 140) and never recovered.

**Decision:** This is forensic work that needs to happen immediately:
1. Check WordPress.org SVN/plugin page change history for May-June 2024
2. Review Google Search Console data for organic traffic changes
3. Check if a plugin update broke something (review support tickets from that period)
4. Search for new TTS competitor launches around that time

**If the cause is fixable** (e.g., bad listing update, SEO regression): fixing it could restore 200+ installs/month — doubling current numbers overnight.

**If the cause is external** (e.g., WordPress.org algorithm change): pivot to alternative distribution channels (direct marketing, content, partnerships).

---

### Decision 5: Launch a "Win-Back" Email Campaign

**Data basis:** 12,475+ uninstalled users with email addresses in the database.

**Decision:** Create a segmented email campaign:
- **Segment A** (left < 6 months ago, reason: bugs/not working): "We fixed X, Y, Z — give us another try"
- **Segment B** (left < 12 months ago, reason: features): "New features: [list]. Come back and check it out"
- **Segment C** (left > 12 months ago): "TTS has changed — here's what's new in 2026"

**Expected impact:** Even a 2-3% reactivation rate = 250-375 reactivated users, which would significantly offset the monthly net loss.

**Important:** Comply with email consent laws (CAN-SPAM/GDPR). Only email users who opted in to communications.

---

### Decision 6: Invest in WordPress.org Listing Optimization

**Data basis:** Installs declined 63% from peak, suggesting visibility/conversion issues on the plugin directory.

**Decision:**
- **Title:** Ensure it includes high-search-volume keywords (e.g., "Text to Speech", "TTS", "Read Aloud", "Audio Player")
- **Short description:** Lead with the benefit, not the feature ("Let visitors listen to your posts" vs "Converts text to audio")
- **Screenshots:** Update with modern, clean screenshots showing the player in action
- **FAQ section:** Add the top 10 support questions to reduce "could not understand" churn
- **Reviews:** Implement an in-plugin review prompt for users who have been active 7+ days with successful audio generation
- **Tested up to:** Always keep this current (latest WP version)

**Expected impact:** WordPress.org SEO is the #1 acquisition channel. Even a 20% improvement in listing conversion could add 20-30 installs/month.

### Decision 7: Create a Pro Conversion Funnel

**Data basis:** TTS Pro has 1 tracked install (likely inaccurate due to opt-in), but even Freemius data likely shows low conversion.

**Decision:** Build a deliberate free-to-pro conversion funnel:

**Revenue target:** If 2% of the ~4,000 active users convert at $59/year = $4,720/year. At 5% = $11,800/year. At 10% = $23,600/year.

## 10. Deep Dive: Investigate June 2024 Install Cliff

**Expected Impact:** Could restore 200+ installs/mo (doubling current numbers)
**Effort:** Low (1-2 days of research)

### What Happened

Installs dropped from 354 (March 2024) to 140 (June) to 91 (July) — a 74% decline in 4 months. The drop was sudden, not gradual, which points to a specific event rather than natural decline.

### Investigation Checklist

#### Step 1: WordPress.org Plugin Page Audit
- [ ] Check SVN commit history for May-June 2024: did a plugin update change the readme.txt?
- [ ] Check if the "Tested up to" WordPress version went stale during that period
- [ ] Check if the plugin was temporarily closed/suspended by WordPress.org for any reason
- [ ] Compare the plugin listing (title, description, tags) before and after May 2024
- [ ] Check if screenshots were broken or removed

#### Step 2: Search Traffic Analysis
- [ ] Check Google Search Console for `text to speech wordpress plugin` and related queries
- [ ] Look for traffic cliff in May-June 2024 — organic search drop would confirm SEO issue
- [ ] Check if WordPress.org changed their search algorithm around that time (community forums)
- [ ] Check referral traffic sources — did any blog/review site stop linking?

#### Step 3: Plugin Update History
- [ ] Review changelog for May-June 2024 releases
- [ ] Check WordPress.org support forum for spikes in complaints during that period
- [ ] Did any update introduce PHP errors, JS errors, or compatibility breaks?
- [ ] Did the plugin stop working with a specific WordPress version released in May/June 2024?

#### Step 4: Competitor Analysis
- [ ] Search WordPress.org for "text to speech" sorted by "most popular"
- [ ] Check if any competitor launched or gained significant traction in May-June 2024
- [ ] Look at competitor install counts on web.archive.org Wayback Machine for comparison
- [ ] Specific competitors to check: BeyondWords, Speaker, GSpeech, ResponsiveVoice

#### Step 5: External Factors
- [ ] WordPress 6.5 was released March 2024 — did it break anything?
- [ ] Google Chrome removed or deprecated any Web Speech API features?
- [ ] Did any major hosting provider block TTS-related functionality?

### Possible Root Causes and Fixes

| Root Cause | Likelihood | Fix | Recovery Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stale "Tested up to" version | High | Update readme.txt to latest WP version | Immediate |
| Broken screenshots/banner | Medium | Upload fresh assets | 1-2 weeks |
| SEO keyword change | Medium | Restore original keywords + optimize | 2-4 weeks |
| Competitor took ranking | Medium | Listing optimization + reviews | 1-3 months |
| WordPress.org algo change | Low | Alternative distribution channels | 3-6 months |
| Plugin update broke installs | Low | Hotfix release | 1-2 weeks |

### What to Do If Cause is Found

- **If fixable (listing/SEO issue):** Fix immediately. This is the highest ROI action — a single readme.txt change could restore 200+ installs/month.
- **If external (algorithm/competitor):** Shift to multi-channel acquisition: direct marketing, YouTube tutorials, blog content, WooCommerce marketplace listing, partnerships with theme developers.

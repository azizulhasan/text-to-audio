The Future of Web Accessibility

Web accessibility is not just a nice-to-have feature. It is a fundamental requirement for building an inclusive internet. According to the World Health Organization, over one billion people worldwide live with some form of disability. Making websites accessible means ensuring everyone can access information regardless of their abilities.

Web accessibility is not just a nice-to-have feature. It is a fundamental requirement for building an inclusive internet. According to the World Health Organization, over one billion people worldwide live with some form of disability. Making websites accessible means ensuring everyone can access information regardless of their abilities.

Text-to-speech technology plays a crucial role in web accessibility. It allows users who are visually impaired, have learning disabilities, or simply prefer audio content to consume information through their ears instead of their eyes. Modern browsers provide the Web Speech API, which includes the Speech Synthesis interface for converting text to spoken words.

The AtlasVoice SDK makes it easy for any developer to add text-to-speech functionality to their website. With just a few lines of JavaScript, you can provide your users with a complete audio experience including play, pause, resume controls, voice selection, and language options. All powered by the voices available on the user's device!
